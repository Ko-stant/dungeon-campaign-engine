# The Three Plagues - Read-Aloud Script

**Last Updated**: 2026-09-28 20:01 EDT

Everything the players hear, in play order. Each passage is meant to become one audio
clip. World, cast and secrets are in [NARRATIVE.md](NARRATIVE.md).

## How this script is written for audio

- **One passage, one clip.** Name each file after its ID (`P0-01.mp3`, `Q2-03.mp3`) so
  clips are easy to find at the table, and so the tracker can play them later.
- **The quoted text is exactly what is spoken.** Numbers are written as words and there
  are no stage directions inside the text. Delivery notes are in each passage's **Voice**
  line.
- **Passages with more than one speaker** are split into lines, one speaker each. Record
  or generate each line separately and join them, or use a tool that supports several
  voices.
- **Pronunciation** of every name is in the cast table in NARRATIVE.md.
- **Quest goals** are table text. Read them out or show them; they are not part of a clip.
- **Sound** notes are optional background suggestions.
- **The Soul Gems do nothing in play** except open the portal. The G passages are flavor
  for quiet moments between fights.

## Prologue

### P0-01 - The lord's request

- **When:** before the first quest.
- **Speaker:** Lord Aldren Voss.
- **Voice:** quiet and serious, in a private study. Tired more than frightened. Slows down
  for "Three plagues walk my lands."
- **Sound:** a fire crackling; rain on a window.

> Come in. Close the door behind you. I would rather my household did not hear this.
>
> You rode through Haldmere to get here, so you have seen it for yourselves. The wells
> run low and taste of iron. The wind has not turned a mill sail in two seasons. The
> forges in Haldmere Cross burn cold, no matter how much coal the smiths feed them. And
> the soil comes up grey behind the plough. My grandfather called this the richest vale in
> the kingdom. My grandchildren will call it a grave, if they are ever born.
>
> My archivist, Brother Tobias, tells me why. Beneath the eastern hills lie the Elemental
> Chambers. Four vaults. Stone, Tide, Gale and Hearth. Long ago the Wardens kept them, and
> their power flowed out into every field and furnace in this land. The Wardens are gone.
> The Chambers have been sealed for longer than anyone can remember. What little power
> seeps past that seal is all we have left, and it is running out.
>
> The seal can be opened. The old books speak of three Soul Gems, the keys to the portal
> that guards the Chambers. Tobias swears there was a verse written beside them, but
> someone scraped half of it from the page long ago. No matter. I know where the gems are,
> because they have made themselves impossible to ignore.
>
> Three plagues walk my lands.
>
> East of the hills, in what were our finest fields, something has woven a blight so thick
> that the crops burst, and the cattle swell and rot where they stand. The farmers call it
> the Blightweaver. That is the first plague. Pestilence.
>
> Beyond the fields, in the old Warden fortress on the Rise, an Ogre Lord called Gorrak
> has grown fat on our herds and our granaries. He is always hungry. That is the second
> plague. Famine.
>
> And above him sits another Ogre Lord, Varnok Ironjaw, whose warband burns every village
> that will not feed the first. That is the third. War.
>
> Each of these beasts carries a Soul Gem. My scouts have seen them shine. I think it is
> the gems that made them what they are.
>
> I cannot send my soldiers. I have too few left, and they are all that stands between the
> ogres and our walls. So I am sending you.
>
> The only road east runs through the Crumbling Halls, the old Warden gatehouse beneath
> Tollen Hill. It is failing, like everything else, so do not linger there. Cross the
> Halls. Find the Bloated Fields. Destroy the three plagues, take their Soul Gems to the
> portal on the Rise, and open the Elemental Chambers, so that their power flows back into
> Haldmere.
>
> Do this, and you will want for nothing for the rest of your days. Fail, and I fear there
> will be no Haldmere left to remember your names.

### P0-02 - At the gate

- **When:** the heroes reach the doors of the Crumbling Halls (right before Q1-01).
- **Speaker:** Maren Ashby.
- **Voice:** brisk and practical; she has done this before and did not like how it went.
- **Sound:** wind; a low groan of settling stone.

> This is as far as the road goes. Past these doors it's Warden stone all the way to the
> east side of the hill. Or it was.
>
> Listen. Hear that? That's the hill settling. It does it more every week.
>
> Lord Voss had me pack what we could spare. It isn't much, so don't waste it.
>
> One more thing. The last patrol we sent in came out with three men instead of five, and
> none of them would say what they saw. Keep your torches lit and your voices low. And
> when you find the far gate, don't look back. Just keep going east.

## Quest 1 - The Crumbling Halls

### Q1-01 - The Crumbling Halls

- **When:** the quest begins.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** hushed, echoing; let the pauses breathe.
- **Sound:** dripping water, trickling dust, distant falling stone.

> The great doors groan shut behind you, and the dark closes in.
>
> The Wardens built this gatehouse to last forever. It is dying anyway. Every step you
> take shakes loose a trickle of dust. Pillars lean like tired old men. Whole passages
> have fallen in, and others, sealed for centuries, have cracked open to the air. The
> stone itself is weakening, as if the earth has forgotten how to hold its shape.
>
> Whatever has made its home down here will not welcome you. Somewhere on the far side of
> these halls is the eastern gate, and beyond it, the Bloated Fields.

**Quest goals**
- Find a way through the Crumbling Halls and leave by the eastern gate.
- *(Optional)* Search for anything the Wardens left behind.

### Q1-02 - The Warden's verse

- **When:** the heroes search the room where the GM placed a Warden's tomb (or any wall
  carving). Clue 2.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** slower; read the verse itself plainly, almost flat, like words cut in stone.

> Carved into the lid of a Warden's tomb, half buried in rubble, are four symbols you know
> from the lord's tale. A mountain for Stone. A wave for Tide. A feather for Gale. A flame
> for Hearth.
>
> Beneath them runs a verse, cut deep and careful, as if whoever carved it wanted it never
> to be forgotten.
>
> Three the plagues that bar the way.
> Three the stones that turn the key.
> But the one who keeps the door
> keeps it still, and waits for thee.

### Q1-03 - The eastern gate

- **When:** the heroes reach the exit.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** relief first, then unease.
- **Sound:** heavy doors; a warm wind; flies.

> Daylight. A thin grey line of it, spilling under a pair of iron-bound doors at the end of
> the hall. The eastern gate.
>
> As you heave the doors open, a warm wind rolls in to meet you. It is heavy, and sweet,
> and wrong, like fruit left far too long in the sun.
>
> Somewhere out there are the Bloated Fields.

## Quest 2 - The Bloated Fields

### Q2-01 - The Bloated Fields

- **When:** the quest begins.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** disgust held under control; slow on "like something breathing."
- **Sound:** buzzing flies, creaking fences, a wet bubbling.

> You step out of the dark into a stink so thick you can taste it.
>
> The Bloated Fields stretch before you under a low, yellow sky. The wheat has swollen and
> split open on the stalk. Fat grey threads drift between the hedgerows, strung from fence
> to barn to tree, and where they touch the ground, the earth heaves and bubbles like
> something breathing. The houses of Millbrook stand empty, their doors webbed shut.
>
> The threads all run one way, towards the heart of the fields. That is where the
> Blightweaver waits, and with it, the first Soul Gem.

**Quest goals**
- Find the Blightweaver's lair and destroy it.
- Claim the first Soul Gem.
- Cross the fields and find the road onward to the Rise.

### Q2-02 - The boy in the cellar (optional)

- **When:** the heroes search the cellar or grain store where the GM hid Tam in Millbrook.
- **Speakers:** Narrator, then Tam Fenwick.
- **Voice (Tam):** a twelve-year-old, whispering, shaking, trying hard not to cry.

**Narrator**

> Behind a stack of rotten grain sacks, something moves. A boy, no more than twelve,
> clutching a pitchfork taller than he is.

**Tam**

> Don't. Don't come any closer.
>
> You're not sick? You're not webbed?
>
> Everyone went to the big tithe barn, in the middle of the fields. The threads took them
> there. Ma too. I heard her calling me. It wasn't her voice, though. Not really.
>
> It's the spider. The big one. It sits in the middle and pulls on the threads, and
> whatever's caught comes to it.
>
> If you're going there, follow the thickest threads. And please. If you find my ma, tell
> her Tam's still hiding. Like she told me to.

### Q2-03 - The tithe barn

- **When:** the heroes first see into the Blightweaver's lair.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** building dread; the last line quick and sharp.
- **Sound:** creaking silk, a slow wet heartbeat.

> Every thread in the fields ends here.
>
> The old tithe barn has split open like an overripe gourd, its beams furred with grey
> silk. The web inside is thick enough to walk on, and it breathes. Shapes hang in it,
> wrapped tight and very still.
>
> At the heart of it all squats the Blightweaver. It is a bloated, many-legged mass the
> size of a hay cart, its skin stretched tight and glistening. Deep inside its swollen
> body, something glows a sickly green. The first Soul Gem.
>
> Its legs begin to twitch along the threads. It knows you are here.

### Q2-04 - The first Soul Gem

- **When:** the Blightweaver dies.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** grim, then quietly hopeful at the end. Clue 3: give the heartbeat a moment.
- **Sound:** a wet collapse; then a soft rain of falling threads.

> The Blightweaver shudders, sags, and bursts, spilling its foul insides across the floor.
>
> In the mess lies a gem the size of a hen's egg, glowing a pale and sickly green. The
> Soul Gem of Pestilence. When you pick it up, it is warm. And for a moment it seems to
> beat against your palm, slow and steady, like a heartbeat. Then it is still. Somewhere
> deep inside it, so faint you cannot be sure, something is wailing.
>
> Outside, a sound like rain begins. It is the webs. All across the fields, the grey
> threads are drying, snapping, and falling to the ground.
>
> The first plague is broken.

### Q2-05 - Smoke on the hills

- **When:** the heroes reach the exit toward the Rise.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** steady; a warning in the last line.
- **Sound:** wind; far-off drums.

> Beyond the last hedgerow, the land climbs toward a line of broken hills. The air is
> cleaner here, but not by much. As the stink of rot fades, the smell of smoke takes its
> place.
>
> On the highest hill you can make out the jagged shape of an old Warden fortress, and
> fires burning all around it. The Rise.
>
> The ogres are waiting.

## Quest 3 - The Wardens' Rise

### Q3-01 - The Wardens' Rise

- **When:** the quest begins.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** low and heavy; this is the enemy's home.
- **Sound:** drums, roaring fires, distant ogre laughter.

> The Wardens' Rise was a fortress once, built to guard the way down to the Elemental
> Chambers. Now it belongs to the ogres.
>
> Their camps sprawl through the cracked courtyards. Gnawed bones. Heaps of stolen grain.
> Burned-out wagons dragged here from the villages of Haldmere. The whole place stinks of
> smoke, and meat, and sweat.
>
> Two Ogre Lords rule here. Gorrak Hollowgut squats in the old granaries, guarding a hoard
> of food he can never finish. Above him, in the great hall, sits Varnok Ironjaw, with his
> Commander and his Champion at his side. Each of them carries a Soul Gem.
>
> And somewhere at the top of the Rise stands the portal to the Elemental Chambers, with
> three empty sockets, waiting.

**Quest goals**
- Defeat Gorrak Hollowgut and claim the second Soul Gem.
- Defeat Varnok Ironjaw, his Commander and his Champion, and claim the third Soul Gem.
- Set all three Soul Gems in the portal and open the Elemental Chambers.

### Q3-02 - The Hollow Merchant (secret)

- **When:** the heroes find Ilsabet's hidden fire near the start of the Rise. Play the
  greeting first; use the other lines when they fit.
- **Speakers:** Narrator, then Ilsabet Crane.
- **Voice (Ilsabet):** dry, amused, never in a hurry. She is not afraid of anything here.
- **Sound:** a small crackling fire; a kettle.

**Narrator**

> Tucked behind a collapsed wall, where no ogre would ever bother to look, a small fire is
> burning. Beside it sits a woman wrapped in a patched grey cloak, her cart piled high with
> sacks, bottles and oddments. She does not look surprised to see you.

**Ilsabet - greeting**

> Well. Visitors who don't smell of goat. That makes a pleasant change.
>
> Sit, if you like. Ilsabet Crane, merchant, at your service, so long as your service has
> coin in it. The ogres leave me be. I sell them salt, and they forget I'm here.
>
> You'll find my prices fair, and my questions few.

**Ilsabet - while they browse**

> Take your time. Nobody up here is in any hurry to die. Except the ones who are.

**Ilsabet - if they ask about the gems or the portal**

> Three stones for three sockets. Tidy, isn't it? Doors like that aren't built to be
> opened. They're built to keep something in, or keep something out. The trick is knowing
> which.

**Ilsabet - farewell** (clue 4)

> Off you go, then. Mind the gate on the east side, it sticks.
>
> And heroes. Every lock has a keeper. Think on that, before you go turning keys.

### Q3-03 - The granary

- **When:** the heroes first see Gorrak.
- **Speakers:** Narrator, then Gorrak Hollowgut.
- **Voice (Gorrak):** slow, thick, chewing between words.
- **Sound:** chewing, creaking hooks, flies.

**Narrator**

> The old granary is heaped to the rafters with the stolen harvest of Haldmere. Sacks of
> grain. Barrels of salt pork. Whole carcasses swinging from hooks.
>
> In the middle of it all, on a throne of flour sacks, sprawls Gorrak Hollowgut. He is
> enormous, even for an ogre, his belly spilling over his belt, grease running down his
> chin. He is still eating. At his throat, on a leather cord, hangs a dull amber stone.

**Gorrak**

> More food. Walked right in. Good.
>
> Gorrak is hungry. Gorrak is always hungry.

### Q3-04 - The second Soul Gem

- **When:** Gorrak dies.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** grim satisfaction; uneasy on the hunger.

> Gorrak Hollowgut topples back into his hoard and does not rise.
>
> You cut the amber stone from its cord. The Soul Gem of Famine is dull and heavy, and cold
> as a winter well. Holding it, you feel a sudden, gnawing hunger, though you ate not long
> ago. And under the hunger, thin and far away, you hear it. A voice, wailing. You put the
> gem away quickly.
>
> Two plagues broken. One to go.

### Q3-05 - The great hall

- **When:** the heroes first see Varnok's hall.
- **Speakers:** Narrator, then Varnok Ironjaw.
- **Voice (Varnok):** calm and cold; he is not impressed. Clue 5 is "The whisper said you
  would come."
- **Note:** written for Gorrak falling first. If the heroes reach Varnok first, drop "and
  the Glutton", and in Q3-04 say "The last plague is broken" instead of "One to go".
- **Sound:** a war camp: whetstones, chains, a crowd falling silent.

**Narrator**

> The great hall of the Rise has become a war camp. Banners of raw hide hang from the
> rafters, and weapons are stacked along every wall.
>
> At the far end, on the Wardens' old stone seat, sits Varnok Ironjaw. His lower jaw is
> capped in black iron, and a blood-red gem burns in the pommel of his sword. At his right
> hand stands Skarr, his Commander, barking orders. At his left, silent and scarred, looms
> Brugg the Unbroken, his Champion.

**Varnok**

> So. The little ones who killed the Weaver and the Glutton.
>
> The whisper said you would come.
>
> Skarr. Brugg. Bring me their heads. Leave the stones.

### Q3-06 - The third Soul Gem

- **When:** Varnok dies.
- **Speakers:** Varnok Ironjaw (dying), then Narrator.
- **Voice (Varnok):** broken, laughing, fading.
- **Note:** Varnok's lines are a strong hint. Skip them to keep the secret tighter.

**Varnok** (optional)

> You think you've won. Fools.
>
> He's been waiting so long. The whisper. He promised.

**Narrator**

> Varnok Ironjaw falls, and the hall goes quiet.
>
> The red gem in his sword pommel pulses once, then steadies. When you pry it loose, the
> Soul Gem of War hums in your hand, and the hum grows louder whenever a blade is drawn
> nearby. Beneath the hum is something else. A thin, furious keening, like a voice that
> has been screaming for a very long time.
>
> You hold all three Soul Gems now. The three plagues are broken.

### Q3-07 - The portal

- **When:** the heroes reach the summit of the Rise. Clue 6. On the map, the portal is
  the locked gate that seals off the Chambers section of map 3; it stays locked until
  Q3-08.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** awe, with a chill on the last sentence.
- **Sound:** high wind; a low hum from the stone.

> At the very top of the Rise, open to the sky, stands the portal.
>
> It is a ring of black stone, taller than three men, carved with the same four symbols you
> saw in the Crumbling Halls. Mountain, wave, feather, and flame. Below them are three
> empty sockets, each the size of a hen's egg.
>
> And above them, at the very top of the ring, is a fifth mark. A Warden's sigil, scratched
> away so savagely that you can barely tell what it once was.
>
> The Soul Gems you carry are pulling now. Not gently any more. They strain toward the
> sockets, and the wailing inside them has become one voice.

### Q3-08 - The gems are set

- **When:** the third gem is placed. This is the reveal.
- **Speakers:** Narrator, then the Dread Wraith, then Narrator.
- **Voice (Narrator):** wonder turning to dread. **Voice (Wraith):** a cold, echoing
  whisper; almost tender.
- **Sound:** grinding stone, three rising tones, then a long inhale.

**Narrator**

> The third gem settles into its socket, and all three flare white. The portal groans
> open.
>
> From beyond it comes a breath of cold air. Then another. And another. As if something
> vast inside has been waiting a very long time to breathe.
>
> The light in the gems gutters, and one by one they go dark. Their glow does not fade. It
> is drawn through the doorway, three streams of pale fire pouring into the Chambers
> beyond.
>
> In the vault of Stone, where the power of the land should burn, a shape rises from the
> floor. It was a man once, robed like a Warden. Now it is a thing of shadow and cold, and
> the light of your three Soul Gems burns in its chest.

**Dread Wraith**

> Three plagues I set to bar this door. And you have brought all three of them back to me.
>
> I am Morvaen, last High Warden of the Four Chambers. And I have been hungry for so very
> long.

**Narrator**

> The Dread Wraith has been waiting for you.

**New quest goal**
- Destroy the Dread Wraith and free the Elemental Chambers.

### Q3-09 - The Wraith's taunts (optional)

- **When:** during the fight, one line at a time, whenever it fits.
- **Speaker:** the Dread Wraith.
- **Voice:** as in Q3-08. Each line is its own short clip (`Q3-09a` to `Q3-09e`).

> **a** (the fight begins) Stone, tide, gale and hearth. All of it mine. All of it.

> **b** (any time) You carried my soul up the mountain for me. I could almost thank you.

> **c** (a hero falls) Another light goes out. The Chambers remember every one.

> **d** (the Wraith is badly hurt) No. Not again. I will not be sealed away again.

> **e** (a spell or blessing is used against it) Your little prayers. I wrote the prayers.

## Soul Gem moments (optional)

Short flavor passages for a quiet moment: a rest, a search, or a hero asking to look at a
gem. They have no game effect.

### G-01 - The gem that wails

- **When:** any time after Q2-04, when a hero holds or studies a gem.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** close and quiet, as if leaning in to listen.
- **Sound:** silence, then a very faint, distant wail.

> In the quiet, you take out the Soul Gem and turn it over in your hand.
>
> Its light moves, slowly, like something swimming just under the surface of dark water.
> You feel the power in it, pressing against your skin, the way you can feel a fire with
> your eyes closed.
>
> And there it is again. A wailing, very far away, as if it came from the bottom of a
> well. The harder you listen, the quieter it gets, until you cannot tell if it was ever
> there at all.

### G-02 - Two gems together

- **When:** the heroes carry two gems (after Q3-04 or Q3-06).
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** uneasy.

> The two Soul Gems do not like being apart. Carried in separate packs, they grow cold.
> Carried together, they warm, and their light seems to lean toward each other.
>
> The wailing is clearer now. Two voices, you would swear, though the longer you listen,
> the more they sound like one voice, answering itself.

### G-03 - The pull of the Rise

- **When:** the heroes carry all three gems and have not yet reached the portal.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** a slow, rising tension.

> With all three Soul Gems together, there is no mistaking it. They pull. Gently, but
> always in the same direction: up, toward the top of the Rise.
>
> Their power hums through your bones, and the wailing inside them has changed. It no
> longer sounds like grief.
>
> It sounds like someone calling you home.

## The End

### E-01 - The Chambers restored

- **When:** the Dread Wraith is destroyed.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** release; let it swell, then settle.
- **Sound:** a scream fading into rushing wind, water, fire; then birdsong.

> The Dread Wraith comes apart with a scream that shakes the Chambers to their roots. The
> three stolen lights tear free of it and burst outward, and the power of the Chambers,
> held back for longer than anyone alive can remember, comes free with them.
>
> The vault of Stone groans and settles, and the cracks in its walls knit shut. In the
> vault of Tide, a dry basin fills with clear, cold water. A wind rises in the vault of
> Gale, with nowhere to go but out and up. And in the vault of Hearth, a fire that has been
> embers for generations roars back to life.
>
> You feel it rush past you, up through the rock and out into the land above.
>
> By the time you climb back into daylight, the wind is turning the mill sails of
> Haldmere.

### E-02 - The lord's thanks

- **When:** the heroes return to Voss Keep.
- **Speaker:** Lord Aldren Voss.
- **Voice:** warmer than in P0-01; he laughs once, near the end.
- **Sound:** a busy courtyard; a forge ringing somewhere.

> You went out to kill three monsters, and you came back having killed a fourth that none
> of us knew existed. I spent my whole life thinking the Wardens simply left. It seems one
> of them never did.
>
> The wells are full. The forges are burning so hot the smiths are complaining about it.
> The farmers say that for the first time in years, the soil smells like soil. Brother
> Tobias wants to know everything you saw in those Chambers, and I think, this time, I
> will let him go and look for himself.
>
> Haldmere owes you its future. I cannot pay a debt like that, but I can make a start. Name
> your reward. And if you have any sense, take a long rest before the next lord comes
> knocking at your door.

### E-03 - Epilogue (optional)

- **When:** last of all.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** a storyteller by a fire; soft.

> The Elemental Chambers stand open now, and the power of the old Wardens runs free across
> Haldmere once more.
>
> Songs are sung about the heroes who broke the three plagues. Fewer are sung about the
> fourth, because few who hear the story believe it. Those who were there know better.
>
> And they say a woman in a grey cloak still sells salt up on the Rise, though there is no
> one left there to buy it.

### X-01 - If the heroes fall

- **When:** the whole party is defeated and the campaign ends there.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** quiet and final.

> The last of you falls, and the dark closes in.
>
> Far away, in a quiet study in Voss Keep, a tired lord waits for news that never comes.
> The wells run dry. The mill sails hang still. And deep beneath the Rise, something
> patient goes on feeding.
>
> The story of Haldmere does not have to end here. But for these heroes, it has.

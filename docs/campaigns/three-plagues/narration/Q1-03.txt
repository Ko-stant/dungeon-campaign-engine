[hushed, a little relieved] Daylight. [pause] A thin gray line of it, spilling under a pair of iron-bound doors at the end of the hall. [pause] The eastern gate.

[unhurried, uneasy] As you heave the doors open, a breath of warm air rolls in to meet you. It is heavy, and sweet, [pause] and wrong, like fruit left far too long in the sun.

[low, unhurried] Somewhere out there are the farms of the Eastmarch.

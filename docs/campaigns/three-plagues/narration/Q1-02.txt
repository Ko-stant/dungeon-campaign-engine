[hushed, unhurried] Carved into the lid of a Warden's tomb, half buried in rubble, are four signs you have seen before, on the cracked fountain at Voss Keep. [pause] A mountain. A wave. A feather. A flame.

[hushed, unhurried] Beneath them runs a verse, cut deep and careful, as if whoever carved it wanted it never to be forgotten.

[slowly, plainly, like words cut in stone] "Three the plagues that bar the way.
Three the stones that turn the key..."

[hushed, unhurried] The last two lines are gone. [pause] Not worn away by time. Gouged out, stroke after stroke, by something sharp, until the stone beneath is scarred and pale. [pause] [low] Someone wanted these words forgotten.

[hushed, unhurried] Of the third line, a single word has survived, near the middle: [pause] one.

[hushed, unhurried] Of the last line, only the final word can still be read: [pause] waits.

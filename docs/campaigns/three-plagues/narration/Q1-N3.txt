[hushed, unhurried] On a statue, sheltered from the dust, someone has drawn in white chalk a circle cut into four parts. In each part is a small sign: a mountain, a wave, a feather and a flame. The sign of the Chapel of the Four. [pause] Beneath it, in a neat and steady hand, are a few lines of writing.

[calm, warm, like a blessing] Sister Wenna and eight of the faithful of the Chapel of the Four passed this way, on the third day of our journey. [pause] The Four walk with us.

[calm, warm, like a blessing] We seek the Wardens' fortress on the Rise, where the records say the way to the Chambers begins. If you are of the faith, pray for us. [pause] [gently] If you are not, take care: while this place is filled with fine treasure, it is also full of devilish traps, some tainted with ancient magics.

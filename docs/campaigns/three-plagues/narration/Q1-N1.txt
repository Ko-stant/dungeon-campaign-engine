[hushed, unhurried] Half buried under a fall of rubble lies what is left of a soldier. A faded blue surcoat, the same as Sergeant Hale's. His legs are pinned beneath a slab of stone, and one hand is still curled around a stub of charcoal. [pause] Inside his helmet, folded small, is a scrap of paper covered in cramped, careful writing.

[hushed, unhurried] It reads:

[hoarse, weary, trying to sound brave, speaking clearly] Tomas Reed, Keep guard. If Sergeant Hale reads this, I'm sorry.

[hoarse, weary, trying to sound brave, speaking clearly] The ceiling came down in the long hall. Aldo was in front of me. [pause] I can't hear him any more. My legs are stuck and I can't feel them. Corporal Brandt says they'll go on to the far gate and come back for me on the way home. I told them to go. [pause] Somebody has to see.

[hoarse, weary, trying to sound brave, speaking clearly] It's been two days, I think. Something moves in the walls when the torch gutters. It doesn't come close while there's light. [pause] I have one torch left.

[hoarse, weary, speaking clearly] Tell my mother I wasn't afraid. [pause] [quieter] Tell her...

[hushed, unhurried] The writing trails off with a long mark, [pause] illegible.

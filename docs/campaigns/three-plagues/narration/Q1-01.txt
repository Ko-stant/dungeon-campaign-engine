[hushed, unhurried] The great doors groan shut behind you, [pause] and the dark closes in.

[hushed, unhurried] The Wardens built this gatehouse to last forever. [pause] But, it is dying anyway. Every step you take shakes loose a trickle of dust. Pillars lean like tired old men. Whole passages have fallen in, and others, sealed for centuries, have cracked open to the air. [pause] The stone itself is weakening, as if the earth has forgotten how to hold its shape.

[hushed, unhurried] Others have come this way before you. Somewhere in these dark halls, some of them, [pause] or at least their bones, [pause] are still here. [low] Whatever has made its home down here will not welcome you either. [pause] Somewhere on the far side is the eastern gate, and beyond it, the Eastmarch.

[hushed, unhurried] With a glance at one another, faces grim with determination, you set off. [long pause] Slow and steady; you will not let this place be the end of you.

[hushed, unhurried] Someone has driven a broken knife into the wood at head height, pinning a sheet of stained paper where no one coming this way could miss it. The words are written in something that has dried a dull brown, [pause] and underlined twice.

[hard, gravelly, ashamed] To whoever comes after. [pause] Turn back.

[hard, gravelly, ashamed] I am Bram Greyford, and I have led my company through six wars. We came east for the lord's gold. There were six of us. [pause] Now there are only four.

[hard, gravelly, ashamed] The farms past this gate are wrong. The crops are swollen and split. The cattle lie in the fields and swell until they burst. There are gray threads strung over everything, fence to barn, barn to tree, and they move when nothing is touching them. Harl went into the big barn at the center after a sound he swore was a child crying. [pause] We heard him scream. [pause] We did not go in after him. Whatever sits in that barn is patient, and it is not alone.

[hard, gravelly, bitter] We are going home. No coin is worth this. [pause] If you have any sense, you will go home too.

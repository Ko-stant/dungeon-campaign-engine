## Quest 3 - The Wardens' Rise

### Q3-01 - The Wardens' Rise

- **When:** the quest begins.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** low and heavy; this is the enemy's home.
- **Sound:** drums, roaring fires, distant ogre laughter.

> The Wardens' Rise was a fortress once, built to guard the way down to the Elemental
> Chambers. Now it belongs to the ogres.
>
> Their camps sprawl through the cracked courtyards. Gnawed bones. Heaps of stolen grain.
> Burned-out wagons dragged here from the villages of Haldmere. The whole place stinks of
> smoke, and meat, and sweat.
>
> Two Ogre Lords rule here. Gorrak Hollowgut squats in the old granaries, guarding a hoard
> of food he can never finish. Above him, in the great hall, sits Varnok Ironjaw, with his
> Commander and his Champion at his side. Each of them carries a Soul Gem.
>
> And somewhere at the top of the Rise stands the portal to the Elemental Chambers, with
> three empty sockets, waiting.

**Quest goals**
- Defeat Gorrak Hollowgut and claim the second Soul Gem.
- Defeat Varnok Ironjaw, his Commander and his Champion, and claim the third Soul Gem.
- Set all three Soul Gems in the portal and open the Elemental Chambers.

### Q3-02 - The Hollow Merchant (secret)

- **When:** the heroes find Ilsabet's hidden fire near the start of the Rise. Play the
  greeting first; use the other lines when they fit.
- **Speakers:** Narrator, then Ilsabet Crane.
- **Voice (Ilsabet):** dry, amused, never in a hurry. She is not afraid of anything here.
- **Sound:** a small crackling fire; a kettle.

**Narrator**

> Tucked behind a collapsed wall, where no ogre would ever bother to look, a small fire is
> burning. Beside it sits a woman wrapped in a patched grey cloak, her cart piled high with
> sacks, bottles and oddments. She does not look surprised to see you.

**Ilsabet - greeting**

> Well. Visitors who don't smell of goat. That makes a pleasant change.
>
> Sit, if you like. Ilsabet Crane, merchant, at your service, so long as your service has
> coin in it. The ogres leave me be. I sell them salt, and they forget I'm here.
>
> You'll find my prices fair, and my questions few.

**Ilsabet - while they browse**

> Take your time. Nobody up here is in any hurry to die. Except the ones who are.

**Ilsabet - if they ask about the gems or the portal**

> Three stones for three sockets. Tidy, isn't it? Doors like that aren't built to be
> opened. They're built to keep something in, or keep something out. The trick is knowing
> which.

**Ilsabet - farewell** (clue 5)

> Off you go, then. Mind the gate on the east side, it sticks.
>
> And heroes. Every lock has a keeper. Think on that, before you go turning keys.

### Q3-03 - The granary

- **When:** the heroes first see Gorrak.
- **Speakers:** Narrator, then Gorrak Hollowgut.
- **Voice (Gorrak):** slow, thick, chewing between words.
- **Sound:** chewing, creaking hooks, flies.

**Narrator**

> The old granary is heaped to the rafters with the stolen harvest of Haldmere. Sacks of
> grain. Barrels of salt pork. Whole carcasses swinging from hooks.
>
> In the middle of it all, on a throne of flour sacks, sprawls Gorrak Hollowgut. He is
> enormous, even for an ogre, his belly spilling over his belt, grease running down his
> chin. He is still eating. At his throat, on a leather cord, hangs a dull amber stone.

**Gorrak**

> More food. Walked right in. Good.
>
> Gorrak is hungry. Gorrak is always hungry.

### Q3-04 - The second Soul Gem

- **When:** Gorrak dies.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** grim satisfaction; uneasy on the hunger.

> Gorrak Hollowgut topples back into his hoard and does not rise.
>
> You cut the amber stone from its cord. The Soul Gem of Famine is dull and heavy, and cold
> as a winter well. Holding it, you feel a sudden, gnawing hunger, though you ate not long
> ago. And under the hunger, thin and far away, you hear it. A voice, wailing. You put the
> gem away quickly.
>
> Two plagues broken. One to go.

### Q3-05 - The great hall

- **When:** the heroes first see Varnok's hall.
- **Speakers:** Narrator, then Varnok Ironjaw.
- **Voice (Varnok):** calm and cold; he is not impressed. Clue 6 is "The whisper said you
  would come."
- **Note:** written for Gorrak falling first. If the heroes reach Varnok first, drop "and
  the Glutton", and in Q3-04 say "The last plague is broken" instead of "One to go".
- **Sound:** a war camp: whetstones, chains, a crowd falling silent.

**Narrator**

> The great hall of the Rise has become a war camp. Banners of raw hide hang from the
> rafters, and weapons are stacked along every wall.
>
> At the far end, on the Wardens' old stone seat, sits Varnok Ironjaw. His lower jaw is
> capped in black iron, and a blood-red gem burns in the pommel of his sword. At his right
> hand stands Skarr, his Commander, barking orders. At his left, silent and scarred, looms
> Brugg the Unbroken, his Champion.

**Varnok**

> So. The little ones who killed the Weaver and the Glutton.
>
> The whisper said you would come.
>
> Skarr. Brugg. Bring me their heads. Leave the stones.

### Q3-06 - The third Soul Gem

- **When:** Varnok dies.
- **Speakers:** Varnok Ironjaw (dying), then Narrator.
- **Voice (Varnok):** broken, laughing, fading.
- **Note:** Varnok's lines are a strong hint. Skip them to keep the secret tighter.

**Varnok** (optional)

> You think you've won. Fools.
>
> He's been waiting so long. The whisper. He promised.

**Narrator**

> Varnok Ironjaw falls, and the hall goes quiet.
>
> The red gem in his sword pommel pulses once, then steadies. When you pry it loose, the
> Soul Gem of War hums in your hand, and the hum grows louder whenever a blade is drawn
> nearby. Beneath the hum is something else. A thin, furious keening, like a voice that
> has been screaming for a very long time.
>
> You hold all three Soul Gems now. The three plagues are broken.

### Q3-07 - The portal

- **When:** the heroes reach the summit of the Rise. Clue 7. On the map, the portal is
  the locked gate that seals off the Chambers section of map 3; it stays locked until
  Q3-08.
- **Speaker:** Narrator.
- **Voice:** awe, with a chill on the last sentence.
- **Sound:** not a breath of wind, even at the summit; a low hum from the stone.

> At the very top of the Rise, open to the sky, stands the portal.
>
> It is a ring of black stone, taller than three men, carved with the same four symbols you
> saw in the Crumbling Halls. Mountain, wave, feather, and flame. Below them are three
> empty sockets, each the size of a hen's egg.
>
> And above them, at the very top of the ring, is a fifth mark. A Warden's sigil, scratched
> away so savagely that you can barely tell what it once was.
>
> The Soul Gems you carry are pulling now. Not gently any more. They strain toward the
> sockets, and the wailing inside them has become one voice.

### Q3-08 - The gems are set

- **When:** the third gem is placed. This is the reveal.
- **Speakers:** Narrator, then the Dread Wraith, then Narrator.
- **Voice (Narrator):** wonder turning to dread. **Voice (Wraith):** a cold, echoing
  whisper; almost tender.
- **Sound:** grinding stone, three rising tones, then a long inhale.

**Narrator**

> The third gem settles into its socket, and all three flare white. The portal groans
> open.
>
> From beyond it comes a breath of cold air. Then another. And another. As if something
> vast inside has been waiting a very long time to breathe.
>
> The light in the gems gutters, and one by one they go dark. Their glow does not fade. It
> is drawn through the doorway, three streams of pale fire pouring into the Chambers
> beyond.
>
> In the vault of Stone, where the power of the land should burn, a shape rises from the
> floor. It was a man once, robed like a Warden. Now it is a thing of shadow and cold, and
> the light of your three Soul Gems burns in its chest.

**Dread Wraith**

> Three plagues I set to bar this door. And you have brought all three of them back to me.
>
> I am Morvaen, last High Warden of the Four Chambers. And I have been hungry for so very
> long.

**Narrator**

> The Dread Wraith has been waiting for you.

**New quest goal**
- Destroy the Dread Wraith and free the Elemental Chambers.

### Q3-09 - The Wraith's taunts (optional)

- **When:** during the fight, one line at a time, whenever it fits.
- **Speaker:** the Dread Wraith.
- **Voice:** as in Q3-08. Each line is its own short clip (`Q3-09a` to `Q3-09e`).

> **a** (the fight begins) Stone, tide, gale and hearth. All of it mine. All of it.

> **b** (any time) You carried my soul up the mountain for me. I could almost thank you.

> **c** (a hero falls) Another light goes out. The Chambers remember every one.

> **d** (the Wraith is badly hurt) No. Not again. I will not be sealed away again.

> **e** (a spell or blessing is used against it) Your little prayers. I wrote the prayers.
